## **1. Preparation**

1. Launch the Drone Technology Virtual Lab environment on your workstation.
2. Select the specific experiment module (from Experiment 01 to 11) you are currently performing.
3. Review the theoretical formulas, AICTE mappings, and learning objectives before beginning.
4. Ensure all virtual test benches and simulation parameters are reset to their default values.
5. Prepare your observation table to securely log independent variables, dependent variables, and sub-calculation outputs.

---

## **2. Simulation Setup & Initialization**

1. Mount the specific virtual component (e.g., Propeller, Frame, ESC, or Battery) onto the active test stand.
2. Select the required input parameters from the component library (e.g., KV rating, material type, airfoil profile).
3. Ensure the test stand is properly calibrated for standard atmospheric conditions (ISA model).
4. Verify that there are no red warning flags on the system dashboard before initiating tests.

---

## **3. Sub-Calculation Execution**

1. Set the required operational conditions (e.g., target RPM, wind speed, throttle percentage, or payload mass).
2. Begin the simulation process gradually to observe the real-time physical effects (e.g., thrust generation, voltage sag, temperature rise).
3. Monitor the live telemetry feeds and graphical outputs during the simulation run.
4. Pause the simulation at the designated target points:
   - Specific throttle percentages (40%, 60%, 80%, 100%)
   - Specific mass increments (0g to 500g)
   - Specific time intervals (for thermal time constants)
5. Stop the simulation once the target metric is achieved.

---

## **4. Data Collection & Verification**

1. Extract the raw numerical data generated by the sub-calculations.
2. Calculate the dependent variables using the governing formulas provided in the theory section.
3. Compare the generated outputs against strict hardware thresholds:
   - Structural safety factor (SF ≥ 2.0)
   - Minimum thrust-to-weight ratio (TWR ≥ 1.5)
   - Maximum thermal limit (≤ 80°C)
4. Flag any parameter that violates the physical safety limits.

---

## **5. Interpretation & Margin Analysis**

1. Use the generated data to plot the characteristic performance curves (e.g., Efficiency vs. Throttle, Drag vs. Velocity Squared, Range vs. Payload).
2. Locate your experimental operating points on the plotted charts.
3. Determine the structural, aerodynamic, or electrical efficiency of your design.
4. Compare theoretical paper predictions against the dynamic simulation outcomes to identify reality-check limitations.

---

## **6. Observations & Analysis**

1. Record all initial parameters and final calculated outputs in your central lab table.
2. Observe critical design trade-offs (e.g., balancing hover stability against aggressive maneuvering, or flight time against payload capacity).
3. Note any non-linear phenomena (like exponential heat rise or quadratic drag curves).
4. Identify the root causes of any system mismatches or thermal runaway risks.
5. Perform a formal contradiction analysis to understand the limitations of the idealized mathematical models versus real-world physics.

---

## **7. Cleanup & Forward Routing**

1. Save the validated configuration parameters directly to your virtual clipboard.
2. Click the 'Unlock Component' button to physically mount the completed subsystem onto the central drone assembly frame.
3. Ensure your verified metrics are securely forwarded to the downstream experiments, as no module can be bypassed.
4. Reset the current test bench, clear the workspace, and safely proceed to the next integrated experiment.
