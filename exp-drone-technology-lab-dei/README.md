### About the Experiment 

This virtual lab consists of a series of 11 interdependent experiments designed to pull students out of textbook theory and drop them directly into a realistic systems engineering workflow. Outputs from earlier experiments feed directly into later ones, enforcing the real engineering design loop as practised in industry.

The 11 experiments covered in this lab are:
1. **Propulsion System Design:** Propeller Geometry, Thrust & Motor Matching
2. **Frame Structural Integrity:** Material Selection, CG Analysis & Arm Stress
3. **Aerodynamic Analysis:** Propeller Blade Airfoil, Drag & Advance Ratio
4. **Power Electronics:** ESC Calibration, PWM Mapping & Dead-Band Verification
5. **Flight Control System:** PID Tuning, Sensor Fusion & Attitude Estimation
6. **Thrust-to-Weight Ratio, Hover Analysis & Payload Capacity Envelope**
7. **Energy Storage System:** LiPo Characterisation, Voltage Sag & C-Rating
8. **Navigation & Positioning:** GPS Accuracy, Barometric Altitude & Error Budget
9. **Thermal Management:** Motor & ESC Temperature Rise, Cooling Requirement
10. **Mission Performance:** Range-Payload Trade-off, Energy Budget & Flight Envelope
11. **INTEGRATED FLIGHT TEST:** Full System Verification & Fly/No-Fly Verdict
