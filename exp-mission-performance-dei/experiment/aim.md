### Aim of the experiment
To pull the propulsion, mass and power-system choices from every earlier experiment into a single **mission-performance model** of the assembled quadcopter: build the U-shaped cruise-power curve from parasitic drag and induced power, find the maximum-endurance speed at its minimum, budget the mission energy (Watt-hours) across a realistic flight phase profile, and map the range-payload envelope that sets how far the craft can fly against how much it carries.
