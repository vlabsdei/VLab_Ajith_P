# Academic References

The cruise-power, endurance-speed and range-payload models in this experiment are based on the following laboratory manual (primary) and prescribed Indian-university textbooks.

**Laboratory manual (primary)**

1. **Malla Reddy College of Engineering & Technology (MRCET), Hyderabad**, *Flight Vehicle Design & Instrumentation Laboratory Manual*. Weight-fraction estimation using the Breguet range formula and the endurance formula (p28).
   https://mrcet.com/pdf/Lab%20Manuals/AE/III-II/FLIGHT%20VEHICLE%20DESIGN%20AND%20INSTRUMENTATION%20LAB.pdf

**Textbooks**

2. **J. D. Anderson, Jr.**, *Introduction to Flight*, McGraw-Hill Education. Power-required curve, maximum-endurance and maximum-range airspeeds.
3. **J. G. Leishman**, *Principles of Helicopter Aerodynamics*, Cambridge University Press. Forward-flight induced-velocity relation for the multirotor cruise-power model.
