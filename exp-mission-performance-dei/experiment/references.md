# Academic References

The cruise-power, endurance-speed, mission-energy-budget and range-payload models implemented in this experiment are derived from the following rotorcraft performance, electric-propulsion and UAV mission-planning literature:

1. **J. G. Leishman**, *Principles of Helicopter Aerodynamics*, 2nd ed. Cambridge, UK: Cambridge University Press, 2006.
   *(Primary source for momentum (actuator-disk) theory in forward flight, the induced-velocity relation that makes induced power fall with airspeed, and the parasitic/induced power decomposition that produces the U-shaped cruise-power curve.)*

2. **W. Johnson**, *Helicopter Theory*. New York, NY: Dover Publications, 1994.
   *(Detailed treatment of induced power, profile power, and the forward-flight power curve, including the minimum-power and minimum-power-required-per-speed operating points used for endurance and range speed.)*

3. **B. W. McCormick**, *Aerodynamics, Aeronautics, and Flight Mechanics*, 2nd ed. New York, NY: Wiley, 1994.
   *(Classical treatment of the aircraft power-required curve, the maximum-endurance and maximum-range airspeeds, and the tangent-from-origin construction used to locate the max-range point on a power curve.)*

4. **R. W. Beard and T. W. McLain**, *Small Unmanned Aircraft: Theory and Practice*. Princeton, NJ: Princeton University Press, 2012.
   *(Source for small-UAV mission-phase energy accounting — takeoff, cruise, loiter/hover and landing segments — and for the electric-propulsion energy-budget approach carried over from the propulsion and mass work of earlier experiments.)*

5. **Quan Quan**, *Introduction to Multicopter Design and Control*. Singapore: Springer, 2017.
   *(Reference for multirotor thrust-to-weight and disk-loading fundamentals, and for the T &prop; W<sup>1.5</sup> scaling of induced power that underlies the range-payload trade-off.)*

6. **Z. Liu, R. Sengupta, and A. Kurzhanskiy**, "A power consumption model for multi-rotor small unmanned aircraft systems," in *2017 International Conference on Unmanned Aircraft Systems (ICUAS)*, Miami, FL, 2017, pp. 310–315.
   *(Electric-multirotor power/energy-consumption model spanning hover, ascent/descent and straight-line forward-flight segments, validated by flight test, supporting the phase-by-phase Watt-hour budgeting used in the Mission Energy sub-experiment.)*

7. **M. Hassanalian and A. Abdelkefi**, "Classifications, applications, and design challenges of drones: A review," *Progress in Aerospace Sciences*, vol. 91, pp. 99–131, 2017.
   *(Reference for payload-fraction and range trends across multirotor scales, and for the practical range-payload envelope trade-offs sized in the final sub-experiment.)*

8. **A. Filippone**, *Flight Performance of Fixed and Rotary Wing Aircraft*. Oxford, UK: Butterworth-Heinemann, 2006.
   *(General aircraft-performance reference for the distinction between maximum-endurance and maximum-range flight conditions, and for climb/descent energy penalties relative to the level-flight (flat-earth) approximation.)*
