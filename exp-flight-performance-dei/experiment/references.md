# Academic References

The thrust-to-weight, hover-throttle and payload-envelope models in this experiment are based on the following laboratory manual (primary) and prescribed Indian-university textbook.

**Laboratory manual (primary)**

1. **Institute of Aeronautical Engineering (IARE), Hyderabad**, *Flight Controls Laboratory Manual* (AAE102 / AAE107). Aircraft performance study — thrust required *Treq = W/(CL/CD)*, endurance and lift/drag coefficients *CL, CD = L/(½ρV²S)* (p19).
   https://www.iare.ac.in/sites/default/files/lab1/AAE107-Flight%20Control%20lab%20manual%20final-converted_0.pdf

**Textbook**

2. **J. D. Anderson, Jr.**, *Introduction to Flight*, McGraw-Hill Education. Thrust-to-weight ratio, hover throttle, and momentum (actuator-disk) hover power *P = T^1.5/√(2ρA)*.
