Theoretical Derivations

01

Thrust-to-Weight Ratio

Total static thrust over the all-up weight (grams-force, g cancels).

TWR = N · T_max / m

02

Hover Throttle & Margin

Thrust rises with throttle squared, so hover sits at √(1/TWR).

hover = √(1/TWR), margin = 1 − hover
—
03

Maximum Payload

Extra mass that still holds the minimum safe thrust-to-weight ratio.

m_pay = N·T_max / TWR_min − m_empty

04

Hover Efficiency

Actuator-disk theory: ideal induced power over the figure of merit.

P = (mg)1.5/(FoM√(2ρA)), eff = m/P
