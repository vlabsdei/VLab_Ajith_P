### Aim of the experiment
To characterise the flight-performance envelope of the assembled quadcopter: compute its thrust-to-weight ratio from the Experiment-1 propulsion and Experiment-2 mass, find the hover throttle and control margin from the quadratic propeller thrust curve, work out the maximum payload that keeps a safe minimum thrust-to-weight ratio, and estimate the hover efficiency (grams of lift per watt) from momentum theory.
