### Aim of the experiment
To design a structurally sound drone frame by balancing the system’s center of gravity (CG) and performing static stress analysis on the structural arms (cantilever beams) to ensure they can withstand thrust and motor weight loads without yielding.

### Learning Objectives:
1. **Balance Mass Distribution**: Position heavy internal components (like the battery pack and payload gear) along the deck rails to calculate and minimize the CG offset coordinate from the geometric center.
2. **Calculate Root Bending Moment**: Determine the shear force and bending moment exerted at the clamped root of the drone arm across varying arm lengths (150 mm, 250 mm, 350 mm) under full thrust.
3. **Evaluate Material Stress**: Compute the flexural bending stress on the outermost fibers of the arm profile and compare it directly against the yield strengths of Carbon Fibre, Aluminium 6061-T6, and Nylon.
4. **Determine Safety Factor (SF)**: Generate a structural safety factor matrix (3 materials × 3 arm lengths) to identify viable design combinations that prevent structural failure mid-flight.
