### Aim of the experiment
To design a structurally sound drone frame by balancing the system's center of gravity (CG) and running static stress analysis on the structural arms (cantilever beams), so they can take the thrust and motor-weight loads without yielding.

### Learning Objectives:
1. **Balance mass distribution.** Position the heavy internal components (battery pack, payload gear) along the deck rails to compute and minimise the CG offset from the geometric center.
2. **Calculate the root bending moment.** Work out the shear force and bending moment at the clamped root of the drone arm across several arm lengths (150 mm, 250 mm, 350 mm) under full thrust.
3. **Evaluate material stress.** Compute the flexural bending stress on the outermost fibers of the arm profile and compare it directly against the yield strengths of Carbon Fibre, Aluminium 6061-T6, and Nylon.
4. **Determine the safety factor (SF).** Build a structural safety factor matrix (3 materials × 3 arm lengths) to find the design combinations that hold up in flight.
