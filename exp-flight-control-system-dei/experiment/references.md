# Academic References

The second-order response, PID-tuning and stability models in this experiment are based on the following laboratory manual (primary) and prescribed Indian-university textbook.

**Laboratory manual (primary)**

1. **Institute of Aeronautical Engineering (IARE), Hyderabad**, *Control Systems Laboratory Manual*. Time response of a second-order system — damping ratio *δ*, natural frequency *ωn*, settling time *Ts = 4/(δωn)* (p7); effect of P/PD/PI/PID on a second-order system (p29–45); Routh's stability criterion (p3–5); root locus, Bode and Nyquist analysis (p53–57).
   https://www.iare.ac.in/sites/default/files/lab1/IARE_Control_Systems_Lab_Manual.pdf

**Textbook**

2. **I. J. Nagrath and M. Gopal**, *Control Systems Engineering*, New Age International. Second-order response, PID control and Ziegler–Nichols ultimate-cycle tuning.
