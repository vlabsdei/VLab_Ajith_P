### Aim of the experiment
To design and tune the attitude-control loop of a quadcopter: model the roll axis as a second-order system, connect the PID gains to the closed-loop natural frequency and damping, work out the step-response metrics (overshoot, rise, settling) and the steady-state error against a disturbance torque, get a first set of gains automatically from the Ziegler–Nichols ultimate-cycle method and then refine them for robustness, and fuse the gyroscope and accelerometer into a drift-free, low-noise attitude estimate with a complementary filter.
