# Academic References

The dilution-of-precision, circular-error and barometric-altitude models in this experiment are based on the following laboratory manual (primary) and textbooks.

**Laboratory manual (primary)**

1. **SRM Valliammai Engineering College**, *Surveying Laboratory Manual* (1903307). Coordinate / position determination using a Total Station (p4–5); Expt 15, traverse and area using a Total Station (p65–66); Expt 16, distance and difference in elevation between two inaccessible points (p67–68).
   https://srmvalliammai.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/1903307-surveying-lab-manual.pdf

**Textbooks**

2. **E. D. Kaplan and C. J. Hegarty (eds.)**, *Understanding GPS/GNSS: Principles and Applications*, Artech House. Geometry (design) matrix, (GᵀG)⁻¹ covariance, HDOP/VDOP/PDOP/GDOP and Circular Error Probable (CEP).
3. **J. D. Anderson, Jr.**, *Introduction to Flight*, McGraw-Hill Education. Standard-atmosphere pressure–altitude (barometric ISA) relation.

*Note: the DOP, CEP and ISA equations are analytical and are not printed in any Indian bench lab manual; they are cited to the textbooks above.*
