# Academic References

The lumped-capacitance thermal model, copper (I<sup>2</sup>R) loss heating, convective and radiative cooling, steady-state winding/ESC temperature, and thermal-time-constant relationships implemented in this experiment are derived from the following heat-transfer and electrical-machines literature:

1. **F. P. Incropera, D. P. DeWitt, T. L. Bergman, and A. S. Lavine**, *Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer*, 7th ed. Hoboken, NJ: John Wiley & Sons, 2011.  
   *(Primary source for the lumped-capacitance transient model, Newton's law of cooling, convective heat-transfer coefficients, and surface radiation exchange used across the thermal sub-calculations.)*

2. **Y. A. Çengel and A. J. Ghajar**, *Heat and Mass Transfer: Fundamentals and Applications*, 5th ed. New York, NY: McGraw-Hill, 2015.  
   *(Covers forced vs. natural convection, the thermal-resistance network representation, and the steady-state temperature balance that sets the winding and ESC operating temperatures.)*

3. **D. C. Hanselman**, *Brushless Permanent Magnet Motor Design*, 2nd ed. Madison, WI: Magna Physics Publishing, 2006.  
   *(Supplies the motor copper (I<sup>2</sup>R) winding-loss model and the winding-temperature limits carried forward from the propulsion experiment.)*

4. **J. H. Lienhard IV and J. H. Lienhard V**, *A Heat Transfer Textbook*, 4th ed. Mineola, NY: Dover Publications, 2011.  
   *(Details transient conduction, the thermal time constant (63.2% response), and radiative-exchange fundamentals underlying the two-node thermal network.)*

5. **R. Remsburg**, *Thermal Design of Electronic Equipment*. Boca Raton, FL: CRC Press, 2001.  
   *(Practical reference for junction-to-ambient thermal resistance, heatsink sizing, and forced-air cooling of power-electronic devices such as the ESC MOSFET bridge.)*
