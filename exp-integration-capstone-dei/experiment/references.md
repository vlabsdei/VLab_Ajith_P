# Academic References

The integrated flight-test capstone re-uses the verified formulas from Experiments 1–10; its references are the laboratory manuals (primary) and prescribed Indian-university textbooks of those experiments.

**Laboratory manuals (primary)**

1. **IARE, Hyderabad** — *Aerospace Propulsion Lab* (thrust, propeller), *Aerodynamics & Propulsion Lab* (Cl, drag), *Control Systems Lab* (PID, second-order), *Flight Controls Lab* (thrust-required, performance).
2. **IIT Madras** — *Strength of Materials Lab* (bending stress, deflection).
3. **Mangalore Institute of Technology (VTU)** — *Engineering Mechanics* (centroid / CG).
4. **SRM Valliammai Engineering College** — *Power Electronics Lab* (I²R, PWM duty ratio); *Surveying Lab* (positioning).
5. **JC Bose University (YMCA), Faridabad** — *Electric Vehicle Lab* (battery charge/discharge).
6. **IIT (ISM) Dhanbad** — *Heat Transfer Lab* (lumped-system transient).
7. **MRCET, Hyderabad** — *Flight Vehicle Design Lab* (Breguet range & endurance).

**Textbooks**

8. **J. D. Anderson, Jr.**, *Introduction to Flight*, McGraw-Hill (propeller, TWR, momentum theory, range/endurance).
9. **I. J. Nagrath and M. Gopal**, *Control Systems Engineering*, New Age International (PID, Ziegler–Nichols).
10. **P. S. Bimbhra**, *Electrical Machinery* / *Power Electronics*, Khanna Publishers (motor BEMF/Kt, MOSFET loss).
11. **R. K. Bansal**, *Strength of Materials*, Laxmi; **G. K. Grover**, *Mechanical Vibrations*, Nem Chand; **R. K. Rajput**, *Heat and Mass Transfer*, S. Chand.
