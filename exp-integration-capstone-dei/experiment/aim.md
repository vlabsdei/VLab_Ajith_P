### Aim of the experiment
To bring everything from Experiments 1–10 together into a single design task: given a fixed **mission specification** (maximum all-up weight, minimum flight time, and a mandatory payload the aircraft has to lift), select and assemble a complete, self-consistent quadcopter (frame, motors, propellers, ESCs, battery, flight controller, receiver and attachments) that meets every requirement at once. When the build fails, the experiment works out **which underlying concept has not landed yet** and names the specific prior experiment to revisit, without solving it for you.
