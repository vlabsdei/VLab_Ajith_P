Theoretical Derivations

01

Required Hover Thrust per Motor

Determined by dividing the total weight of the drone by the motor count (N_motors = 4).

T_req = (M_total * g) / N_motors
—
02

Aerodynamic Thrust per Motor

Calculated using blade momentum theory at the motor's operating speed in revolutions per second (n = RPM / 60).

T_aero = C_t * ρ * n² * D⁴
