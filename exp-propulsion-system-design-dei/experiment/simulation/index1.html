Design Note: The internal voltage drop (V_drop) steals power directly from your battery. If you choose a propeller that is too heavy, V_drop spikes, leaving less Back-EMF to spin the motor. If V_bemf drops too low, your drone won't achieve the RPM needed to take off.

02

Actual Loaded RPM

The motor only spins based on the remaining Back-EMF voltage:

n_loaded = KV × V_bemf

Design Note: Motor efficiency defines your flight time. High waste heat (I²R) rapidly drains the battery and can physically melt the motor mid-flight. Always design your drone so its required Hover Thrust falls near the peak of the efficiency curve.
