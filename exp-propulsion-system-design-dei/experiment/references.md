# Academic References

The thrust, propeller-geometry and motor-matching models in this experiment are based on the following laboratory manual (primary) and prescribed Indian-university textbooks.

**Laboratory manual (primary)**

1. **Institute of Aeronautical Engineering (IARE), Hyderabad**, *Aerospace Propulsion Laboratory Manual* (AAE108 / AAEB12). Propeller test-rig experiment — measurement of propeller thrust and efficiency (thrust vs RPM / inlet velocity), p48–49.
   https://www.iare.ac.in/sites/default/files/lab1/AERO_AEROSPACE_PROPULSION_LABORATORY_LAB_MANUAL.pdf

**Textbooks**

2. **J. D. Anderson, Jr.**, *Introduction to Flight*, McGraw-Hill Education. Propeller thrust/power coefficients, advance ratio *J = V/(nD)*, and propeller efficiency.
3. **P. S. Bimbhra**, *Electrical Machinery*, Khanna Publishers. Motor back-EMF and torque-constant relations (KV / Kt) for the brushless-motor matching model.
